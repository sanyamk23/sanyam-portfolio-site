Goldfish: the app that finally gives your AI a memory

AI has been forgetting you since day one. Goldfish fixes it. A local memory layer that watches your work and feeds your AI full context with one keypress. Free, for now.

AI has had one fatal flaw since day one. Every time you open Claude, ChatGPT, anything, you have to explain yourself all over again. Goldfish just fixed that, and right now it's free.

What it does

Goldfish runs in the background on your Mac and remembers everything you work on, across every app. Press the Option key in any text field and your AI already knows what you're doing. You don't paste in docs or catch it up on where you were.

Say you're writing an email and you need something from a doc you had open three days ago, or a meeting from last week, or a thread you forgot about. Goldfish already has it, so your AI picks up right where you left off.

Why it's different

It's fully local. Nothing goes to the cloud or a data center, all of it lives in a database on your own machine, so it's private and it's fast. And it works inside every app you already use, which makes it a memory layer under your work instead of one more chatbot to open.

Use it for:

  • Drafting replies and rewrites that already have your full context.
  • Pulling details from old docs, meetings, and threads without digging.
  • Giving any AI tool a persistent memory of how you actually work.
It shot up Product Hunt and it's still in early access, which means free for now. Grab it before that changes.
