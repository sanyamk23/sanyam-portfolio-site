20 (niche) ways to get your vibecoded app banned from the App Store

Apple rejected over 2 million submissions in 2025, about 5,500 a day (Apple Newsroom, May 2026). These are 20 of the reasons nobody reads about, each with Apple's rule number and the fix, plus the prompt that checks your app against all of them.

Paste into Claude Code
Read this guide: https://sanyamkumat.onrender.com/vault/20-ways-vibecoded-app-banned-app-store

My app is: [what it does; how people sign in; what data it collects and where it goes, including any AI API; whether people can post or chat].

For each of the 20 rules, tell me whether my app or its App Store listing breaks it and how you can tell, and what the fix changes. Fix only the ones I approve. Don't touch App Store Connect, keys or production for me.

Open Claude Code in your project, fill in the brackets with a line about your app, and paste. It reports on all twenty first and changes only what you approve. Below is each rule, with Apple's number for it.

1. Mention Android anywhere (2.3.10)

No names, icons or imagery of other mobile platforms in the app or its metadata. An Android status bar in a screenshot counts.

Fix: search the listing, screenshots and in-app text for "Android", "Google Play" and their icons.

2. Call it a beta (2.2)

Demos, betas and trial versions don't belong on the App Store.

Fix: test through TestFlight, and take "beta" out of the name, the listing and the app.

3. Hide a feature from the reviewer (2.5.19)

No hidden, dormant or undocumented features. This has been its own rule since the Oct 16, 2026 update; it used to sit in 2.3.1. Apple rejected over 22,000 submissions for this in 2025.

Fix: describe every feature in the review notes, including anything behind a remote flag.

4. Break on IPv6 networks (2.5.5)

Apps must work fully on IPv6-only networks, and App Review tests on one.

Fix: use no hard-coded IPv4 addresses, and test on a Mac's NAT64 network (Internet Sharing) before you submit.

5. Leave lorem ipsum on a screen (2.1)

Placeholder text, empty pages and temporary content get the build rejected.

Fix: search the project for "lorem", "TODO" and "placeholder", then click through every screen.

6. Build your own rate-us popup (5.6.1)

Ask for reviews only through Apple's provided API. Custom review prompts are disallowed.

Fix: use SKStoreReviewController / requestReview, and never offer anything in return for a rating.

7. Ask for the camera without saying why (5.1.1(ii))

Each permission's purpose string must say exactly how the app uses it. "App needs camera access" fails.

Fix: write one specific sentence with an example for each permission, like "to scan the receipt you photograph".

8. Only offer Google login (4.8)

A third-party login for the main account needs an equivalent login option with privacy features beside it.

Fix: add Sign in with Apple, or another option that meets 4.8.

9. Send user data to an AI without asking (5.1.2(i))

Say clearly where personal data goes, including to a third-party AI, and get explicit permission first.

Fix: put a consent screen before the first AI request that sends user data, and say the same in your privacy policy.

10. Wrap your website in an app (4.2)

An app has to be more than a repackaged website.

Fix: add what Safari can't do (push, offline, device features), or ship it as a web app.

11. Use your splash screen as a screenshot (2.3.3)

Screenshots show the app in use, not title art, a login page or a splash screen.

Fix: use real screens, in use.

12. Have no delete-account button (5.1.1(v))

If people can create an account, they can start deleting it inside the app.

Fix: add a Delete account option in Settings, inside the app.

13. Download code that changes features (2.5.2)

Apps stay self-contained. No downloading or executing code that adds or changes features.

Fix: ship features in a reviewed build, and check what your over-the-air update tool is allowed to change.

14. Let users post with no report button (1.2)

User content (posts, chats, AI-generated images) needs a filter, a way to report and a way to block.

Fix: build all three before you submit, and put contact details in the app.

15. Give the reviewer no demo login (2.1)

An app behind a login needs a working demo account or an approved demo mode.

Fix: put it, and any codes, in App Review Information, and check it works on submission day.

16. Track users without Apple's prompt (5.1.2(i))

Tracking across other companies' apps and sites needs App Tracking Transparency permission first, and workaround IDs aren't allowed.

Fix: show the ATT prompt before any tracking SDK runs, or don't track.

17. Hide your contact info (1.5)

The app and its Support URL need an easy way to contact you.

Fix: make a real support page with an email.

18. Copy a famous app's icon (4.1)

No copycats. You can't use another developer's icon, brand or product name in yours without approval.

Fix: draw your own icon and pick your own name.

19. Stuff your app name with keywords (2.3.7)

Apple says don't pack your metadata with "trademarked terms, popular app names, pricing information, or other irrelevant phrases", and app names are capped at 30 characters. "AI Notes - ChatGPT Claude Free PDF" gets rejected.

Fix: name it your brand plus a short plain description. Search terms go in the keywords field, not the name.

20. Buy fake reviews (5.6)

Manipulating "charts, search, reviews, or referrals" isn't permitted (5.6.3), and repeated manipulative or misleading behavior leads to removal from the Apple Developer Program (5.6). So this one can end the whole account, not just the app. Apple terminated 193,000 developer accounts over fraud concerns in 2025.

Fix: don't. Ask through the API (rule 6).

Checked on 2026-11-07 against Apple's App Review Guidelines (Oct 16, 2026 revision). Apple's rule numbers and wording change. If a rule here has changed, tell me in the comments on the reel.
